BUYER NOTES

Questions before a demonstration inquiry.

Is this live inventory?

No. Vehicles, prices, statuses and preparation records are fictional interface material and are not an offer.

Does the form send information?

No. It validates in this browser and displays local feedback only.

Can the record be tailored for a destination?

The concept shows where a buyer’s destination requirements would be reviewed before instruction.
