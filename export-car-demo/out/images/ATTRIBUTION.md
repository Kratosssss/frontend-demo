# Image and font sources

## Vehicle images

All vehicle images in this concept demo are original, local, AI-generated concept assets created on 2026-08-12 with OpenAI image generation. They contain no intended vehicle manufacturer marks, model names, readable plates, people, business contacts or offer information. They are illustrative only and do not represent inventory, a manufacturer or an actual shipment.

The input prompts explicitly required generic unbranded vehicles and no logos, readable plates, text, watermarks, letters or numbers. Final assets were manually reviewed at their exported crops before use.

- `hero.jpg`, `harbor-s7.jpg`, `northline-e5.jpg`: container-port export-yard lineup, wide negative space for the dossier masthead.
- `workland-p9.jpg`: generic utility pickup at a container loading bay.
- `civicline-4.jpg`, `transit-m8.jpg`, `cityloop-c2.jpg`: generic silver sedan and pale-blue hatchback in an inspection-lane setting.

The applicable [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/) state that, as between the user and OpenAI and to the extent permitted by law, the user owns the output. This project bundles the generated files locally and makes no remote image request or hotlink.

## Typography

The local `../fonts/` files are official IBM Plex webfont builds from the [IBM Plex repository](https://github.com/IBM/plex), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete license is bundled as `../fonts/OFL.txt`. Files retained locally: IBM Plex Sans Regular/SemiBold, IBM Plex Sans Condensed Regular/SemiBold, and IBM Plex Sans Arabic Regular/SemiBold.
